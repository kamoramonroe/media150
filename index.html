Creative Statement

I am a current student of the Media Studies Program here at Hunter college. For as
long as can remember, I have been an artist, and art has been something that has
always driven my passions as I aged. Some part of me tried to push it away, convinced
that it was the noble thing to do so I could have a stable career—but I believe that there
is no reason to be alive if you are not living doing what you love. My first artistic
discipline was like anyones: visual art, and it still remains my main one today.
Even so, I have a deep love, almost obsession—for music and performing. I go to
sleep dreaming of the stage and wake up with music in my lungs. Not only that, I adore
writing—so much so that copyediting was my first formal work experience, in which I
became the first ever high school student to be published with the Brooklyn Rail.

Years have passed since that time, but I will still forever mention that publication in
everything I do, even in my own personal statement. I believe I was put on this earth
to create things, and to do so in the name of vulnerability, and in the name of fostering
community, a community of individuals that have a lust for life and a lust for learning—like
myself. In everything I do, I wish to be great, and most of all—I want everyone to see it.
