With "AI Slop" distorting our reality...-Nesrine Malik

The article explores the rise of AI-generated "slop" and its role in creating a distorted digital reality that has both political and commercial impacts. This media, often being low-quality synthetic media, is said to promote regressive social hierarchies and fascist aesthetics, and they do this by leveraging the biases of being trained on historic data, which allows for this data to promote harmful ideas rooted in racism, white supremacy, xenophobia, and more. Because these images and videos are democratized and inherently seen as “truth,” they can bypass traditional fact-checking channels and software, which allows them to appear in places where fewer checks are done, for example, WhatsApp, a common messaging platform. Then, what becomes harmful is that these pieces of media are then able to gain unearned credibility and rapport from those viewing them. Social media platforms prioritize this content because it drives high user engagement, and because this content is captivating to users, regardless of its accuracy or human cost. Ultimately, the constant influx of artificial imagery is constantly working to desensitize those who are viewing it, making it increasingly difficult for individuals to distinguish objective truth from fantasy. This saturation leads individuals to be placed in a state of political and social paralysis, not allowing them to be able to see the harm done in both areas.
