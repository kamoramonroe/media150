The Black Soul

The Black Soul is an exploration on black bodies, and how they are policed in such a precarious way from life to death. It explores the predatory nature present in much of Black Anthropology; and the predatory nature that is integrated into much of our cultural practices, many of which rooted in the christian religion and how black individuals tend to experience it. There is much to be said about the timeline in which a black girl can be just that: a black girl. How long can she go without her body being policed, without it being seen as a weapon. How long can she go without being questioned for not conforming, on the other hand. The same goes for black boys, too, but I have not lived that tale, so I cannot speak on it as well. The question of what black femininity is, and what it means is something I hope to explore, as well. It is an interesting intersection that lies within blackness and femininity; something wildly different than typical adaptations of femininity. It is something entirely unique, haunting, and attempted by many.

I used mainly duotone filters and color adjustments for my png images. I did not remove or cut any parts of the image away, or use much cropping, either. All I did, was make some of the images into PNGS without backgrounds, or adjust the black/white scale to make it so that the background wouldn't be as visible, then layered images on top of one another strategically in order for the focus to remain on different curated aspects of the images.
